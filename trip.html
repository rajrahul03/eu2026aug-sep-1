  • Bar Bambino – Legendary milk bar (ul. Hoża 19)
  • Must try: Pierogi, Żurek (sour rye soup), Bigos (hunter's stew)

💡 Milk bars (Bar Mleczny) are government-subsidized canteens from communist era – amazing food for 15-25 PLN!

💡 Poland is incredibly affordable – you can eat like a king for €5-10 per meal

  • Nowy Świat street – Elegant shopping & dining street
  • Copernicus Science Centre – Interactive science museum (35 PLN)

💡 Palace of Culture observation deck on 30th floor has the best views – "best view because you can't see the building itself!"

💡 Warsaw Rising Museum is emotionally heavy but essential for understanding Warsaw's history

💡 Łazienki Park is free and has free Chopin concerts on Sundays in summer at the monument
